Ga. Code · Title 53

§ 53-12-243. Trustee's duty to inform

A trustee shall keep the qualified beneficiaries of the trust reasonably informed about the administration of the trust and of the material facts necessary for them to protect their interests.

Under the 2019 amendment, the duty to inform extends to remainder beneficiaries, not only current beneficiaries, whenever a material change affects the trust corpus.

Absent a contrary provision, the trustee shall respond to a beneficiary's request for information within a reasonable time.
